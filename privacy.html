Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Who we are

Task Message is a messaging app that lets you chat with people and turn messages into tasks. In this policy, "we" means the operator of Task Message. Contact: moshebriefme@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your phone number (to sign in), your name, and optional "About" text.
  • Messages and attachments: encrypted on your device before they are sent. We store only the encrypted data and cannot read it.
  • Tasks, chat names, group members and stages: stored so the app can work across your devices.
  • Contacts: if you allow it, your phone contacts are read on your device to find people who use Task Message. Your address book is not uploaded; we compare phone numbers only.
  • Device token: to send you notifications.
  • Location: only when you choose to share it in a chat, and only with that chat.
  • Reports: if you report a message or person, the report and the reported message are sent to us for review.

How we use it

To run the app: sign you in, deliver messages and notifications, show tasks to your team, keep the service safe, and act on reports. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who we share it with

Service providers that host and run the app for us: Supabase (database and file storage), Twilio (sign-in codes by text message), Google Firebase (notifications), and Render (website hosting). They process data only to provide their service.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete my account. This deletes your profile, contacts, settings and devices and removes you from your chats. Messages you already sent remain with the people who received them.

Your choices

You can turn off read receipts, block or report people, deny contacts, location, camera or microphone access in your phone's settings, and delete your account.

Children

Task Message is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes.
