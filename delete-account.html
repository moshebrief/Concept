Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Delete your Task Message account

Task Message

Delete it in the app

  • Open Task Message.
  • Tap Settings.
  • Tap Delete my account.
  • Type DELETE and tap Delete.

No longer have the app?

Email moshebriefme@gmail.com from any address with the subject "Delete my Task Message account" and the phone number you signed up with. We will confirm by text message and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profile (name, photo, About), phone number, contacts matches, settings, devices and notification tokens, and your membership in all chats. Your encrypted messages and files stored for you are deleted.

What is kept

Messages you already sent stay with the people who received them, because they are on their phones. Reports you made or that were made about you may be kept for up to 90 days to keep the service safe.
